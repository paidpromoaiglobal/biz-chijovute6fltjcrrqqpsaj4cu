Located conveniently on Vivekananda Road near Ambicapatty, we have been serving the people of Silchar and surrounding areas with elegant, affordable, and customized decoration solutions for weddings, receptions, engagements, and all kinds of social celebrations. Our experienced team brings creativity and dedication to every event we undertake.
